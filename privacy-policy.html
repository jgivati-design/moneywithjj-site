Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Who operates this app

This app is operated by money with JJ.com ("we," "us," "our"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at moneywithjj.com@gmail.com.

What data we access, collect, process, and store

This app connects to your own Pinterest and/or TikTok account(s) to publish content on your behalf. Specifically:

  • Pinterest: with the pins:read, pins:write, and boards:read scopes, we can read your boards and create new pins on a board you configure. We do not read, modify, or delete any of your existing pins.
  • TikTok: with the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes, we can read your basic profile information (display name, avatar) and post videos to your account.
  • OAuth credentials: when you authorize the app, Pinterest/TikTok issue an access token and refresh token, which we store so the connection persists without requiring you to log in again for every post.

We do not access, collect, or store any data belonging to other Pinterest or TikTok users, and we do not collect analytics, cookies, or tracking data from any website visitor. This app operates on behalf of a single connected account per platform.

Why we use this data

The data above is used solely to publish video/pin content you've created to your own connected Pinterest and/or TikTok account(s), automating a task you would otherwise do manually through each platform's own app or website.

How long we retain data, and how to request deletion

OAuth tokens are retained only for as long as your account stays connected. You can end this at any time by:

  • Revoking the app's access directly from your Pinterest or TikTok account settings, or
  • Emailing moneywithjj.com@gmail.com to request that we delete any data we hold about your connection.

Once revoked or deletion is requested, we delete the stored tokens and stop making any further requests to that account.

How we protect data

OAuth tokens are stored in a private location on our own server, not publicly accessible, and are never logged, displayed, or included in any output the app produces. Access to the server itself is restricted to the app's operator.

Whether we share data with third parties

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party. The only parties your data is ever sent to are Pinterest's and TikTok's own APIs, directly, as part of publishing the content you've authorized.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this same page.
